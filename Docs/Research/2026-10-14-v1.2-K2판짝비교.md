## 3. 명령 · 자리

```bash
python3 -I src/measure/compare.py --protocol src/measure/protocol-g4.json --set mem/g4/dev-cmp-g4.jsonl \
  --base K=runs/g1-K-s1/g4/eval-dev-cmp-g4,…,runs/g1-K-s5/g4/eval-dev-cmp-g4 \
  --new K2=runs/g1-K2-s1/g4/eval-dev-cmp-g4,…,runs/g1-K2-s5/g4/eval-dev-cmp-g4 \
```
